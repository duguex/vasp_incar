20 Molecular dynamics

This section is for users who want to study time evolution or finite-temperature behavior by first-principles molecular dynamics. First decide whether your purpose corresponds to NVE, NVT, or NPT, and then read only the relevant subsection. It is helpful to understand in advance the roles of the input settings and of outputs such as .ene, .md, and .md2.

In OpenMX Ver. 4.0, the following seven types of molecular dynamics simulations can be performed.

  • •

    Microcanonical molecular dynamics (NVE)

  • •

    Constant-temperature (NVT) molecular dynamics by velocity scaling (NVT_VS)

  • •

    Constant-temperature (NVT) molecular dynamics with velocity scaling applied independently to each atom (NVT_VS2)

  • •

    Constant-temperature (NVT) molecular dynamics by the Nose-Hoover method (NVT_NH)

  • •

    Multi-heat-bath molecular dynamics (NVT_VS4)

  • •

    NPT molecular dynamics by velocity scaling and the Parrinello-Rahman method (NPT_VS_PR)

  • •

    NPT molecular dynamics by velocity scaling and the Wentzcovitch method (NPT_VS_WV)

In conventional empirical molecular dynamics, interatomic forces are given by functions containing parameters, whereas in first-principles molecular dynamics, an electronic-structure calculation is carried out at each MD step, and the forces acting on atoms are evaluated directly. Therefore, it is characterized by the ability to treat processes accompanied by the formation and breaking of chemical bonds. The details of each molecular dynamics simulation are described below.
