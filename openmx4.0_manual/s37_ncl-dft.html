37 Non-collinear DFT

Non-collinear DFT calculations are available in OpenMX. The most general scheme without any restriction on the spin-quantization axis at each real-space grid point is implemented. Spin-orbit interaction [8, 9, 10, 11, 16] can also be included self-consistently. To perform a non-collinear DFT calculation, set the keyword scf.SpinPolarization as follows.

   scf.SpinPolarization        NC        # On|Off|NC

In this case, the Kohn-Sham orbitals are represented by two-component spinors. The initial spin orientation at each site is given as follows.

  <Atoms.SpeciesAndCoordinates           # Unit=Ang
    1  Mn    0.00000   0.00000   0.00000   8.0  5.0  45.0 0.0 45.0 0.0  1 on
    2  O     1.70000   0.00000   0.00000   3.0  3.0  45.0 0.0 45.0 0.0  1 on
  Atoms.SpeciesAndCoordinates>

   1:    Sequential number of atom
   2:    Atomic species
   3:    x coordinate (or normalized coordinate along the a axis)
   4:    y coordinate (or normalized coordinate along the b axis)
   5:    z coordinate (or normalized coordinate along the c axis)
   6:    Initial occupation for the up-spin state
   7:    Initial occupation for the down-spin state 
   8:    Euler angle of the local field for the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention): theta
   9:    Euler angle of the local field for the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention): phi
         The 8th and 9th Euler angles are also used to generate the initial spin density
  10:    Euler angle of the local field for the orbital magnetic moment (OpenMX convention): theta 
  11:    Euler angle of the local field for the orbital magnetic moment (OpenMX convention): phi
  12:    When one of the following keywords, "scf.Constraint.NC.Spin", "scf.NC.Zeeman.Spin",
         or "scf.NC.Zeeman.Orbital", is specified, enter "1" to apply the constraint scheme
         to the corresponding atom, and "0" otherwise.
  13:    For the LDA(GGA)+U method, enter "on" to promote orbital polarization, and "off" for a normal calculation.

The initial Euler angles (θ, ϕ) for the orientations of the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) and the orbital magnetic moment are given in the 8th, 9th and 10th, 11th columns, respectively. The 12th column is a switch for the constraint method, which adds a constraint functional for the orientations of the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) and the orbital magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) at each atomic site. Here, 1 means that the constraint functional is applied, while 0 means that no constraint is applied. For details of DFT calculations with constraints on the orientation of the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention), see Section 41, “Constraint DFT for non-collinear spin orientation.” The last 13th column is a switch for the method that promotes orbital polarization in the LDA(GGA)+U method. Use on to promote orbital polarization and off for a normal calculation. Figure 37 shows a vector representation of the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) in an MnO molecule calculated by the non-collinear DFT method. This calculation can be reproduced using the input file Mol_MnO_NC.dat in the directory work. The following two files can be used to visualize the non-collinear spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) in real space.

   System.Name.nc.xsf
   System.Name.ncsden.xsf

Here, System.Name is the user-specified System.Name.
System.Name.nc.xsf and System.Name.ncsden.xsf are vector files in the XSF format supported by XCrySDen. The former contains the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) projected onto each atom by Mulliken analysis, while the latter contains the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) on the real-space grid. These two files can be visualized using XCrySDen’s ’Display → Forces’, as shown in Figure 37.

The spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) and its Euler angles for each atom, calculated by Mulliken analysis, are stored in the file System.Name.out as follows.

***********************************************************
***********************************************************
                   Mulliken populations
***********************************************************
***********************************************************

   Total spin moment (muB)   4.998503440   Angles (Deg) 44.991211107  -0.000000002

               Up       Down      Sum      Diff        theta      phi
    1   Mn   9.59803  4.76902  14.36705   4.82901    44.99208   -0.00000
    2    O   3.40122  3.23173   6.63295   0.16950    44.96650   -0.00000

 Sum of MulP: up   =    12.99925 down          =     8.00075
              total=    21.00000 ideal(neutral)=    21.00000

Here, the spin magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) output by OpenMX has the opposite sign to the physical magnetic moment of the electron. This is true not only for non-collinear calculations but also for the treatment of up-spin and down-spin in collinear calculations, so that the spin orientation in the OpenMX convention is opposite to the orientation of the physical magnetic moment of the electron. Similarly, the orbital magnetic moment (OpenMX convention) output by OpenMX also has the opposite sign to the physical orbital magnetic moment of the electron. For the orbital magnetic moment, see also Section 39, “Orbital magnetic moment.”

In general, SCF calculations in the non-collinear DFT method are less easy to converge than collinear calculations. This is because the energy change associated with a change in spin orientation is very small. The constraint scheme imposing constraints on the orientations of the spin magnetic moment and the orbital magnetic moment can be effective in accelerating the convergence of SCF calculations. Spin-orbit interaction is supported in the non-collinear DFT method, but not in the collinear DFT method. For spin-orbit interaction, see Section 38, “Relativistic effects.”
