31 Charge Doping

Electron and hole doping can be performed using the keyword scf.system.charge.

    scf.system.charge     1.0     # default=0.0

Positive and negative signs correspond to hole and electron doping, respectively. Fractional charge doping is also possible. Since FFT is used in OpenMX to solve the Poisson equation, the excess charge given by the keyword scf.system.charge is neutralized by a uniform background charge with the opposite sign. Therefore, when comparing the total energies among different charge states, note that the total energies include an additional electrostatic interaction induced by the background charge. The correct way to treat charged isolated systems is discussed in Sec. 61.

As an example, Fig. 34 shows the spin density of a carbon nanotube (with a finite length of 14 Å) in the cases of hole doping, neutral, and electron doping. The total spin moments of the neutral and electron-doped nanotubes are 1.0 and 2.2, respectively, whereas the total spin moment of the hole-doped nanotube is almost zero. In the neutral and electron-doped nanotubes, spin polarization occurs due to dangling bonds at the nanotube ends.
