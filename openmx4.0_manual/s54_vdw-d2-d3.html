54 DFT-D2 and DFT-D3 methods for vdW interactions

The DFT-D2 method [148] and the DFT-D3 method [149, 150] proposed by Grimme can be used to incorporate vdW interactions. In conventional local density approximation (LDA) and generalized gradient approximation (GGA), long-range dispersion interactions may not be described sufficiently. In the DFT-D approach, in order to compensate for this deficiency, an empirical dispersion correction term is added to the total energy obtained by Kohn-Sham DFT, and the total energy is conceptually treated as Etot=EKS−DFT+Edisp. Here, Edisp is given as a sum of pairwise −Cn/Rn-type interactions multiplied by a damping function, which corrects the long-range dispersion force while avoiding double counting in the short-range region. While the DFT-D2 method mainly uses coefficients that depend on the atomic species, the DFT-D3 method uses coefficients that reflect the local coordination environment, and therefore generally offers better transferability and accuracy. Consequently, for systems in which vdW interactions are important, such as molecular crystals, layered materials, and molecular adsorption systems, the DFT-D approach provides a practical correction scheme.
