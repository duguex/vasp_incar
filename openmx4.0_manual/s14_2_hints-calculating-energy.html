14.2 Hints for Calculating Energy Curves of Bulk Systems

When calculating the energy curve of a bulk system as a function of the lattice constant, discontinuities may appear in the energy curve. In general, this discontinuity originates from changes in the number of real-space grid points used for integration as the lattice constant changes. As an example, Fig. 7 shows the energy curve of bcc-Fe. When the cutoff energy is fixed (for the cases of 200 and 290 (Ryd)), we can see that the energy curve becomes discontinuous (although not very pronounced). The discontinuous jumps in energy occur at lattice constants where the number of computational grid points changes. To avoid discontinuities in the energy curve, the keyword scf.Ngrid is available.

    scf.Ngrid     32 32 32    # n1, n2, and n3 for a-, b-, and c-axes

When the number of real-space grid points is explicitly specified by this keyword, each unit-cell vector is discretized by the grid numbers given here, without referring to the cutoff energy specified by the keyword scf.energycutoff. As can be seen from Fig. 7, a smooth curve is obtained in the calculation with the grid fixed at 32×32×32.
