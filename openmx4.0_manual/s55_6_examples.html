55.6 Unfolding input example

Input files for eight calculation examples are included in files for eight calculation examples are included in.

  • •

    SiC_Primitive.dat

    An example for analyzing the pseudo-atomic-orbital composition of each band for the primitive cell of perfect two-dimensional honeycomb SiC (collinear, non-spin-polarized).

  • •

    SiC_C_NSP_P.dat

    An example of the band unfolding method for a (2×2) supercell of perfect two-dimensional honeycomb SiC (collinear, non-spin-polarized).

  • •

    SiC_C_SP_V.dat

    An example of the band unfolding method for a (2×2) supercell of two-dimensional honeycomb SiC with an Si vacancy (collinear, spin-polarized).

  • •

    SiC_NC_SO_P.dat

    An example of the band unfolding method for a (2×2) supercell of perfect two-dimensional honeycomb SiC (non-collinear calculation including spin-orbit interaction).

  • •

    SiC_NC_SO_V.dat

    An example of the band unfolding method for a (2×2) supercell of two-dimensional honeycomb SiC with an Si vacancy (non-collinear calculation including spin-orbit interaction).

  • •

    BCC_Fe_Perfect.dat

    An example for analyzing the pseudo-atomic-orbital composition of each band for the primitive cell of perfect bcc Fe (collinear, spin-polarized).

  • •

    BCC_Fe_C_SP_Perfect.dat

    An example of the band unfolding method for a cubic cell of perfect bcc Fe. (collinear, spin-polarized)

  • •

    BCC_Fe_NC_SO_Disorder.dat

    An example of the band unfolding method for a cubic cell of bcc Fe with structural disorder (non-collinear calculation including spin-orbit interaction).
