46.9 NEGF method for non-collinear DFT

OpenMX Ver. 4.0 supports the NEGF method combined with non-collinear DFT. All functionalities of the non-collinear DFT method and the NEGF method are implemented in a mutually consistent manner. All functions, including spin-orbit interaction, the DFT+U method, and the constraint method for the directions of the spin magnetic moment and orbital magnetic moment, are compatible with the NEGF implementation. Therefore, it is expected that a wide range of problems can be treated, such as electron transport through domain walls with helical magnetic structures. The procedure for running an NEGF calculation is basically the same as in the collinear DFT case.

As an example of an NEGF calculation combined with non-collinear DFT, Fig. 51 shows the calculated transmission of a zigzag graphene nanoribbon. The spin magnetic moments at the zigzag edges are aligned upward and rightward in the left and right electrode regions, respectively. The electrode calculations were carried out in Step 1 by a non-collinear band-structure calculation using the constraint method for the spin magnetic moment. In the Step 2 calculation, no constraint was imposed. As a result of the Step 2 SCF calculation, it is found that the spin direction in the central region gradually rotates, as shown in Fig. 51 (a). This calculation can be reproduced by the input files Lead-L-8ZGNR-NC.dat, Lead-R-8ZGNR-NC.dat, and NEGF-8ZGNR-NC.dat stored in the work/negf_example directory. Input files for non-collinear NEGF calculations of a one-dimensional gold chain are also stored in the same directory and may be used as a reference.
