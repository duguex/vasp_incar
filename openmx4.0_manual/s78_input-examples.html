78 Included input examples

For reference, the dat files corresponding to the calculation examples in this manual are stored in the directory work. The list of input files is given below.

  Molecules or clusters
  C60.dat            SCF calc. of a C60 molecule
  C60_DC.dat         DC calc. of a C60 molecule
  C60_LO.dat         Low-order scaling calc. of a C60 molecule
  CG15c_DC.dat       DC calc. of DNA
  Cr2_CNC.dat        Constrained DFT calc. of a Cr2 dimer
  Doped_NT.dat       SCF calc. of a doped carbon nanotube
  H2O.dat            Geometry opt. of a water molecule
  MCCN.dat           DC calc. of a multiply connected carbon nanotube
  Methane2.dat       Geometry opt. of a distorted methane molecule
  Methane.dat        SCF calc. of a methane molecule
  Methane_OO.dat     Orbital optimization of a methane molecule
  Methane_ED.dat     Total energy decomposition of a methane molecule
  Mn12.dat           SCF calc. of a single-molecule magnet, Mn12
  Mol_MnO_NC.dat     Non-collinear SCF calc. of a MnO molecule
  Nitro_Benzene.dat  SCF calc. of nitrobenzene under an electric field
  Pt13.dat           SCF calc. of a Pt13 cluster
  Pt63.dat           SCF calc. of a Pt63 cluster
  SialicAcid.dat     SCF calc. of a sialic acid molecule
  Valorphin_DC.dat   DC calc. of a valorphin molecule
  Valorphin_MO.dat   Molecular orbital calc. of a valorphin molecule
  Benzene_RCP.dat    Geometry opt. of a benzene molecule for RCP/FLPQ analysis

  Ionization potentials and electron affinities
  H2O+0.dat   SCF calc. of neutral H2O for ionization-potential evaluation
  H2O+1.dat   SCF calc. of H2O (+1 charge) for ionization-potential evaluation
  C2H2+0.dat  SCF calc. of neutral C2H2 for ionization-potential evaluation
  C2H2+1.dat  SCF calc. of C2H2 (+1 charge) for ionization-potential evaluation
  C2H4+0.dat  SCF calc. of neutral C2H4 for ionization-potential evaluation
  C2H4+1.dat  SCF calc. of C2H4 (+1 charge) for ionization-potential evaluation
  O2+0.dat    SCF calc. of neutral O2 for ionization-potential evaluation
  O2+1.dat    SCF calc. of O2 (+1 charge) for ionization-potential evaluation
  CO+0.dat    SCF calc. of neutral CO for ionization-potential evaluation
  CO+1.dat    SCF calc. of CO (+1 charge) for ionization-potential evaluation
  OH-0.dat    SCF calc. of neutral OH for electron-affinity evaluation
  OH-1.dat    SCF calc. of OH (-1 charge) for electron-affinity evaluation
  O2-0.dat    SCF calc. of neutral O2 for electron-affinity evaluation
  O2-1.dat    SCF calc. of O2 (-1 charge) for electron-affinity evaluation
  Cl2-0.dat   SCF calc. of neutral Cl2 for electron-affinity evaluation
  Cl2-1.dat   SCF calc. of Cl2 (-1 charge) for electron-affinity evaluation
  CN-0.dat    SCF calc. of neutral CN for electron-affinity evaluation
  CN-1.dat    SCF calc. of CN (-1 charge) for electron-affinity evaluation
  SiH-0.dat   SCF calc. of neutral SiH for electron-affinity evaluation
  SiH-1.dat   SCF calc. of SiH (-1 charge) for electron-affinity evaluation

  XPS and core-hole examples
  C2H2.dat          Initial-state calc. of C2H2 for XPS
  C2H2-CH.dat       Final-state calc. of C2H2 for XPS
  TiC216.dat        Initial-state calc. of TiC for XPS
  TiC216-CH3.dat    Final-state calc. of TiC for XPS
  Si-4-SOI.dat      Initial-state calc. of Si for XPS
  Si-4-CH-SOI1.dat  Final-state (j=3/2) calc. of Si for XPS
  Si-4-CH-SOI6.dat  Final-state (j=1/2) calc. of Si for XPS
  N1Be2_IS.dat      Initial-state calc. of NBe2 for XPS with ESM
  N1Be2_FS.dat      Final-state calc. of NBe2 for XPS with ESM

  NEB and molecular dynamics
  C2H4_NEB.dat      NEB calc. of a C2H4 dimer
  Si8_NEB.dat       NEB calc. for hydrogen diffusion in Si
  Gly_NH.dat        Nose-Hoover MD of a glycine molecule
  Gly_VS.dat        Velocity scaling MD of a glycine molecule
  Si_NPT_VS_PR.dat  NPT MD of bulk Si by the velocity-scaling + Parrinello-Rahman scheme
  Si_NPT_VS_WV.dat  NPT MD of bulk Si by the velocity-scaling + Wentzcovitch scheme

  Bulk solids
  Cdia.dat              SCF calc. of bulk diamond
  MnO_NC.dat            Non-collinear SCF calc. of bulk MnO
  FeO_NC.dat            Non-collinear SCF calc. of bulk FeO
  CoO_NC.dat            Non-collinear SCF calc. of bulk CoO
  NiO_NC.dat            Non-collinear SCF calc. of bulk NiO
  NiO-cFLL.dat          LDA+U calc. of NiO by the cFLL scheme

  DIA8_DC.dat           DC calc. of bulk diamond including 8 atoms

  DIA216_DC.dat         DC calc. of bulk diamond including 216 atoms

  DIA8-VA.dat           Virtual-atom SCF calc. of diamond

  GaAs.dat              Non-collinear SCF calc. of bulk GaAs
  NaCl.dat              SCF calc. of bulk NaCl
  NaCl_FC.dat           SCF calc. of bulk NaCl with a Cl-site vacancy
  Si8.dat               Geometry opt. of distorted bulk Si
  Si8-pV.dat            Enthalpy opt. of bulk Si under 10 GPa
  Si8-LNO.dat           O(N) DC-LNO calc. of bulk Si including 8 atoms
  NdCo5_4f.dat          E vs. lattice-constant calc. of NdCo5 including Nd 4f states
  NdCo5_4f+U.dat        E vs. lattice-constant calc. of NdCo5 including Nd 4f states with +U
  NdCo5_OC.dat          E vs. lattice-constant calc. of NdCo5 with an Nd open-core pseudopotential
  FePt.dat              SCF calc. of L1_0-FePt within collinear DFT
  FePt-NC-SCF.dat       SCF calc. of L1_0-FePt within non-collinear DFT
  FePt-NC.dat           One-shot diagonalization of L1_0-FePt with SOI
  Mnfcc-EvsLC.dat       E vs. lattice-constant calc. of fcc Mn
  Si.dat                Example input generated from a CIF file by cif2omx

  Al111_WorkFunc_0E.dat  Work-function calc. of Al(111) with 0 empty-atom layers

  SiC_Primitive_BD.dat   Spin-texture analysis of SiC by the BandDispersion scheme
