64.1 “Lattice constant vs. energy” curves

By setting the following keywords, one can calculate a lattice constant vs. energy curve.

  MD.Type                     EvsLC      #
  MD.EvsLC.Step                0.4       # default=0.4%
  MD.maxIter                   32        # default=1
  MD.EvsLC.flag              1 1 1       # default=1 1 1
                                         # (0: fixed, 1:expansion, -1:contraction)

If the keyword MD.Type is set to EvsLC, the total energy can be calculated while sequentially changing the unit-cell vectors a, b, and c. The modification of the unit-cell vectors is carried out by uniformly expanding or contracting the initial vectors by a constant ratio specified by MD.EvsLC.Step. The number of calculation steps is specified by the keyword MD.maxIter. If one wishes to fix part of the lattice vectors, the keyword MD.EvsLC.flag can be used. The default setting is 1 1 1, which means that uniform expansion/contraction is applied along the a-, b-, and c-axes. If this flag is set to 0, the corresponding axis is fixed at its initial value. On the other hand, setting it to -1 means uniform contraction. If the calculation finishes normally, the file System.Name.EvsLC is generated. Even during the calculation, the intermediate results are written to the file System.Name.EvsLC.

Here, System.Name means System.Name. The columns in System.Name.EvsLC are arranged in the following order: ax, ay, az, bx, by, bz, cx, cy, cz in units of Å, and the total energy in units of Hartree. Here, a⁢(b,c)x, a⁢(b,c)y, and a⁢(b,c)z are the x-, y-, and z-components of the vector a(b,c), respectively.
