60.5 XPS input example

For reference, four examples of input files are provided in the directory work.

  • •

    C2H2.dat, C2H2-CH.dat

    Calculation of the absolute binding energy of the 1s state of a carbon atom in a gaseous C2H2 molecule

  • •

    TiC216.dat, TiC216-CH3.dat

    Calculation of the absolute binding energy of the 1s state of a carbon atom in metallic TiC crystal (a supercell containing 216 atoms)

  • •

    Si-4-SOI.dat, Si-4-CH-SOI1.dat, Si-4-CH-SOI6.dat

    Calculation of the absolute binding energies of the 2⁢p3/2 and 2⁢p1/2 states of a silicon atom in crystalline silicon

  • •

    N1Be2_IS.dat, N1Be2_FS.dat

    Calculation of the N-1⁢s absolute binding energy in the two-dimensional material NBe2: an example calculation combined with the ESM method

Other calculation examples using this functionality and the input files used in the calculations are available at the website: https://www.openmx-square.org/vps_pao_core2019/ for reference.
