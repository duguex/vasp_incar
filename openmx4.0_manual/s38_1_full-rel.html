38.1 Fully relativistic treatment

Within the framework of the pseudopotential method, fully relativistic effects including spin-orbit interaction can be taken into account in non-collinear DFT calculations [12, 35, 16]. On the other hand, the treatment of spin-orbit interaction is not supported in collinear DFT calculations. The fully relativistic effects can be taken into account through the following two steps.

(1) Generation of j-dependent pseudopotentials
First, generate j-dependent pseudopotentials using ADPACK. For user convenience, j-dependent pseudopotentials for many elements are available in Database Ver. 2019 [162]. For details on how to generate j-dependent pseudopotentials, please refer to the ADPACK manual.

(2) SCF calculation
If a j-dependent pseudopotential is specified in the setting of keyword Definition.of.Atomic.Species, spin-orbit interaction can be introduced by the following keyword scf.SpinOrbit.Coupling. 

    scf.SpinOrbit.Coupling      on         # On|Off, default=off

Spin-orbit interaction is not treated as a perturbation, but is incorporated self-consistently within the framework of the pseudopotential method. Through this spin-orbit interaction, the α and β spin components in the two-component spinor interact directly with each other. In the implementation, the j-dependent nonlocal pseudopotential couples the two-component spinor, so SOC is not a small correction added afterward; rather, it contributes from the same origin to band splitting, magnetic anisotropy, orbital magnetic moments, and the stability of non-collinear magnetic structures. In non-collinear DFT calculations, the spin orientation in real space has a definite meaning only when spin-orbit interaction is included. In the absence of spin-orbit interaction, the relative spin orientation with respect to the molecular or crystal orientation is energetically degenerate, and the spin orientation in real space is not uniquely determined. However, the relative orientation between spins (for example, ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic configurations) has physical meaning even without spin-orbit interaction and can lead to energy differences, so please do not confuse these two points.
