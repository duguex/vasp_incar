25 Orbitally decomposed total energy

In OpenMX Ver. 4.0, it is possible to decompose the total energy into contributions from individual basis functions. This is performed by projection onto the basis functions [73]. The orbitally decomposed total energy can be calculated simply by adding the following keyword.

    Energy.Decomposition      on        # on|off, default=off

As an example, the orbitally decomposed total energy for a methane molecule is shown below.

     % mpirun -np 5 openmx Methane_ED.dat > met_ed.std &

Here, Methane_ED.dat is stored in the work directory. After the calculation finishes, met_ed.out is obtained, and the following information is written out.

One can see that the total energy is decomposed into contributions from each atomic orbital of each atom. This functionality is useful when discussing how local perturbations caused by impurity atoms or vacancies affect the stabilization/destabilization of neighboring atoms. It is also expected to be useful for analyzing the physical picture underlying a wide range of phenomena. However, the release of this functionality is still experimental at present. We plan to further develop this functionality in the near future.
